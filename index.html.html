CyberSeva is a working prototype of ScamSaathi AI. It reads what a scammer is asking you to do, predicts the next move, helps you verify who is really calling, rebuilds the incident as evidence, and builds a recovery plan for you.

One incident, six steps, about five minutes.

The demo incident

Priya in Bengaluru gets an SMS that her SBI KYC expires today. Minutes later a caller who says he is from the bank's security team tells her the account will be blocked. He asks for an OTP, has her install a remote app and enter her UPI PIN. ₹9,800 leaves her account. The next day, a "recovery cell" offers to return it for a fee.

All names, numbers and accounts here are invented sample data.

This prototype runs entirely in your browser. A rule-based engine stands in for the language model and OCR that the full product would use, so results on your own text are indicative, not a guarantee. ScamSaathi guides and prepares you. It cannot reverse a transaction or promise recovery of money.
